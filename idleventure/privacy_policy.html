Privacy Policy for Idleventure

Last updated: July 2026

Thank you for playing Idleventure.

This Privacy Policy explains how Idleventure handles user data.

1. Data Collection

Idleventure is a primarily offline single-player game.

Idleventure is designed to minimize data collection and only collects anonymous technical information when required to improve stability, gameplay, balance, and user experience.

The game does not intentionally collect, store, transmit, or share personal data such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Payment information
  • Login credentials

Idleventure does not require account creation, registration, or user login.

2. Player Nickname

Players may choose a nickname during onboarding for gameplay purposes.

This nickname:

  • is chosen locally by the player
  • is stored only on the device
  • is used only inside the game

This nickname is not transmitted to external servers.

3. Local Save Data

Idleventure stores game progress locally on the user's device.

Examples include:

  • Player progress
  • Experience and levels
  • Inventory and equipment
  • Talents and upgrades
  • Pets and achievements
  • Settings and preferences
  • Daily reward state
  • Selected nickname

Idleventure uses local storage mechanisms such as:

  • SharedPreferences
  • DataStore
  • Local database storage, if used by the application

Game progress is currently stored locally and is not uploaded to cloud save services.

Important: Since progress is currently stored only locally, uninstalling the application, clearing app data, or factory resetting the device may permanently delete game progress.

Players may also export and import their local save data using the built-in backup feature. Backup files are created and restored only at the user's request and remain stored locally unless the user chooses to share them.

4. Local Notifications

Idleventure may display local notifications on the user's device, such as gameplay reminders, reward availability, or progression-related alerts.

These notifications are generated locally on the device using Android system notification features and do not require sending personal data to external servers.

Users can disable notifications at any time through their Android device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Idleventure uses the following third-party services provided by Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage information to help improve gameplay, game balance, stability, and overall user experience.

Examples of collected information may include:

  • Game progression events
  • Feature usage
  • Session duration
  • General gameplay statistics
  • Device and app information necessary for analytics

Firebase Analytics does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects technical diagnostic information whenever the application encounters unexpected errors.

Crash reports may include:

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • Application version
  • Crash stack traces
  • Diagnostic logs
  • Technical gameplay context, such as hero level, current zone, or current class, solely for debugging purposes

This information is used exclusively to identify bugs and improve application stability.

For more information about Google Firebase, please visit:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Google Play Services

Idleventure relies on standard Google Play Services available on supported Android devices.

Some Firebase functionality may require Google Play Services to operate correctly.

Google Play Services are governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

7. Future Online Features and Save Migration

Idleventure currently operates as an offline single-player experience.

Future updates may introduce optional online features such as:

  • Cloud save
  • Online multiplayer
  • Leaderboards
  • Competitive events

If such systems are introduced, existing local save data may require migration, adjustment, balancing, or separation from future online progression to ensure fair gameplay and prevent cheating.

The developer may apply progression balancing, migration systems, or compensation rewards when major gameplay systems are updated.

8. Ads and In-App Purchases

Idleventure currently includes:

  • No advertisements
  • No in-app purchases

If advertisements or optional in-app purchases are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features become available.

9. Children's Privacy

Idleventure is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Data Security

Most gameplay progress remains stored locally on the user's device.

Anonymous analytics and technical crash reports may be securely processed by Google Firebase to improve game stability, balance, and user experience.

No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected by Idleventure.

11. Data Retention

Anonymous analytics and crash diagnostics are retained according to Google Firebase's data retention policies.

Local gameplay data remains stored on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, removed by uninstalling the application, or removed by clearing the application's storage.

12. Your Choices

You may:

  • Disable notifications at any time through your Android device settings.
  • Delete your local save data using the in-game options or by clearing the application's storage.
  • Uninstall the application at any time.

Some analytics collection may also be limited through your device's privacy settings, where supported.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if future features include:

  • Cloud save
  • Online multiplayer
  • Additional analytics services
  • Ads
  • In-app purchases
  • Remote push notifications

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed.
